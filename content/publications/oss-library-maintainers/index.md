We proposed and validated three community-driven metrics designed to help open-source library maintainers better understand how their libraries are used in the wild.

**Key contributions:**

- Three novel metrics derived from community signals (e.g., issue discussions, dependency graphs, and usage patterns).
- Empirical validation demonstrating that these metrics correlate with maintainer-relevant decisions around prioritization and deprecation.
- A tool prototype that surfaces these metrics to maintainers in a digestible form.
